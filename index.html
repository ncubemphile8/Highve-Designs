01 / About Highve Designs
A South African architectural practice

Architecture with purpose.

Highve Designs is a South African architectural practice established in 2017, providing innovative architectural design, project management and contract administration services to clients across the public and private sectors.

Our work spans residential developments, educational facilities, healthcare infrastructure, commercial buildings and government developments. We deliver practical, sustainable and contextually responsive solutions that balance creativity with technical excellence.

THE HIGHVE APPROACH
Architecture should not only meet functional requirements — it should contribute positively to communities, improve quality of life and create spaces that remain relevant for generations.
